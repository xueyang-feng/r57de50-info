r57de50 Production

A personal application for archiving trading records and monitoring a privately operated trading system.

What the application does

The application copies trading audit files, system-status records, historical research files, and account snapshots between the operator’s Google Drive folders and a Google Cloud virtual machine. It uses these records to build private monitoring and post-trade analytics dashboards.

Google Drive authorization

The operator authorizes Google Drive access through a custom Google OAuth client used by rclone. The configured workflow reads research and audit records and creates or updates archive and status files. The granted Drive scope may permit broader file access than these configured folders; access follows the permissions approved on Google’s consent screen.

Personal use

This application is maintained for the operator’s personal use. It does not offer public account registration or investment services. The public pages describe the application; trading records, account balances, and private dashboards are not published here.
